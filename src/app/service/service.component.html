Our Services

We offer a range of professional electrical services designed to meet your needs. Explore the services we specialize in below:

Main Panel Upgrade

Our experts provide professional main panel upgrades to ensure your home or business has a safe and reliable electrical system. We upgrade old panels to meet current electrical codes, ensuring safety and efficiency.

EV Charger Installation

We offer installation of electric vehicle (EV) chargers for your home or business. Our team ensures the charger is installed safely and meets all necessary electrical requirements, so you can charge your vehicle efficiently and reliably.

Lighting

Our lighting services include installation of indoor and outdoor lighting systems, energy-efficient solutions, and custom lighting designs to enhance the look and feel of your space while minimizing energy consumption.

Tesla Powerwall Installation

We specialize in installing Tesla Powerwall systems, helping you harness the power of renewable energy. Our team ensures a seamless installation, allowing you to store solar energy for use during outages or at night.

Rough Wiring

Our rough wiring services are essential for new construction or renovation projects. We ensure that all wiring is installed correctly and safely before walls are closed up, providing a solid foundation for all your electrical systems.
